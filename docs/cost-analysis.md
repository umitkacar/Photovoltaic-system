- System size (economies of scale)

## 📈 ROI & Payback Analysis

### Payback Period Formula

$$\text{Simple Payback (years)} = \frac{\text{Net System Cost}}{\text{Annual Savings}}$$

$$\text{Net System Cost} = \text{Total Cost} - \text{Incentives}$$

$$\text{Annual Savings} = (\text{kWh/year} \times \text{Electricity Rate}) + \text{Demand Charge Savings}$$

---

### Residential ROI Example

**System**: 6 kW in California

**Inputs**:
- Installed cost: $18,000
- Federal ITC (30%): -$5,400
- **Net cost**: $12,600
- Annual production: 9,000 kWh
- Electricity rate: $0.28/kWh (CA average)
- Annual savings: 9,000 × $0.28 = $2,520

**Payback**:
$$\text{Payback} = \frac{12,600}{2,520} = 5.0 \text{ years}$$

**ROI (25-year)**:
- Total savings: $2,520 × 25 = $63,000
- Net profit: $63,000 - $12,600 = $50,400
- ROI: $(50,400 / 12,600) × 100 = 400\%$
- **Annualized ROI**: 20%/year

### Advanced Financial Metrics

#### Net Present Value (NPV)

$$NPV = \sum_{t=1}^{25} \frac{C_t}{(1 + r)^t} - C_0$$

Where:
- $C_t$ = Cash flow in year $t$ (savings)
- $r$ = Discount rate (typically 5-8%)
- $C_0$ = Initial investment

**Example** (6 kW residential):
- Initial cost: $12,600
- Annual savings: $2,520
- Discount rate: 6%
- 25-year NPV: **$19,800**

**Interpretation**: Positive NPV = good investment

---

#### Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

**Definition**: Discount rate where NPV = 0

**Typical Solar IRRs**:
- Residential (good solar resource): **12-18%**
- Commercial (with demand charges): **15-25%**
- Utility-scale (PPA): **8-12%**

**Comparison**:
- S&P 500 average: ~10%/year
- Municipal bonds: 3-5%/year
- Solar: **13.5% average** (outperforms most investments!)

## 🎁 Incentives & Tax Credits

### Federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC)

**Status (2025)**: **30% tax credit** on total system cost

**Deadline**: Expires **December 31, 2025** (steps down to 26% in 2026)

**Example**:
- System cost: $20,000
- 30% ITC: $20,000 × 0.30 = **$6,000 credit**
- Net cost: $14,000

**Eligibility**:
- ✅ Owned systems (cash or loan)
- ✅ Residential, commercial, industrial
- ✅ Off-grid and grid-tied
- ❌ Leased systems (credit goes to leasing company)

## 💳 Financing Options

### Cash Purchase

**Pros**:
- ✅ Maximum savings (no interest)
- ✅ Full 30% ITC benefit
- ✅ Immediate ownership

**Cons**:
- ❌ High upfront cost
- ❌ Long payback period

**Best for**: High net-worth individuals, businesses with cash reserves

---

### Solar Loan

**Types**:
1. **Secured loan** (home equity): 4-7% APR
2. **Unsecured loan**: 6-10% APR
3. **PACE financing** (Property Assessed Clean Energy): 5-8% APR

**Example** (6 kW, $18,000 system):
- Loan amount: $18,000
- Term: 15 years
- APR: 7%
- **Monthly payment**: $161.70
- **Total paid**: $29,106

**Comparison**:
- Cash: $12,600 net (after ITC)
- Loan: $29,106 total → $20,406 net (after $8,700 ITC applied to principal)

**Savings**: Monthly utility bill reduction ($200) > loan payment ($162) = **$38/month positive cash flow**

---

### Lease

**Structure**:
- $0 upfront
- Monthly lease payment: $100-200 (for 6 kW)
- 20-25 year term
- Leasing company owns system

**Pros**:
- ✅ $0 upfront
- ✅ Maintenance included
- ✅ Immediate savings

**Cons**:
- ❌ No ITC benefit (goes to leasing company)
- ❌ Total cost higher than cash/loan
- ❌ Complications if selling home

**Best for**: Low upfront capital, renters (with long-term lease)

---

### Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)

**Structure**:
- $0 upfront
- Pay per kWh: $0.10-0.15/kWh (lower than utility)
- 20-25 year term

**Example**:
- Utility rate: $0.18/kWh
- PPA rate: $0.12/kWh
- Annual production: 9,000 kWh
- **Savings**: (0.18 - 0.12) × 9,000 = **$540/year**

**Pros**:
- ✅ $0 upfront
- ✅ Immediate savings
- ✅ No maintenance responsibility

**Cons**:
- ❌ No ITC benefit
- ❌ Lowest total savings
- ❌ PPA rate escalator (typically 1-3%/year)

1. **Residential**: $2.50-3.00/W ($12,600-18,000 net for 6 kW)
2. **Commercial**: $1.80-2.50/W
3. **Labor**: 10-20% of total cost
4. **Batteries**: +$700-1,200/kWh (lithium)

### ✅ ROI

1. **Payback**: 5-15 years (average 7.1)
2. **ROI**: 13.5% annualized (outperforms S&P 500)
3. **Lifetime savings**: $31,000-150,000

### ✅ Incentives

1. **Federal ITC**: 30% (expires Dec 31, 2025!)
2. **MACRS**: 26% additional benefit (commercial)
3. **State incentives**: Vary widely ($0-10,000+)

### ✅ Financing

1. **Cash**: Maximum savings, long payback
2. **Loan**: Positive cash flow from day 1
3. **Lease/PPA**: Lower savings, but $0 upfront
